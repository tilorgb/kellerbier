# Alpha update: the Wald is open

Build: `ae241e3`

Floor 3 is playable, and a lot of the rough edges from the last round are smoothed off.

## New

- **Floor 3, Der Wald, is playable.** A new floor with its own tileset, rooms and lantern-dark spots, reached by playing through from the start.
- **New creatures:** Kaninchen, Bachforelle, Boar, Borkenkäfer and Specht, each with its own way of moving and attacking.
- **A new mini-boss, Bieber**, and **a new boss at the end of the floor.**
- Creatures on every floor now have front, back and side views.
- New sampled sound effects for animal deaths and for cleansing poison.

## Changed

- Föhn no longer bends enemy shots; it still pushes yours.
- A subtle cue when your Promille tier changes.
- Die Große Kellerassel is tougher.
- Settings: the exit button works, sliders behave, Fire is no longer a key binding, and the Promille readout is fixed.
- Placed bombs survive shots and ignore push/pull items.
- Floor 3 rooms spawn only the Wald's own creatures.

## Known

- The Boarisch intro text for the new boss is a draft and has not been checked by a native speaker.
