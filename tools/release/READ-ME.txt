KELLERBIER
A Bavarian cellar-crawling roguelike bullet hell.


HOW TO PLAY IT

  Double-click Kellerbier.html. That is the whole installation.

  It is one self-contained file — the game, its art and its music are all
  inside it — so it runs straight off your disk with nothing to install and
  no internet connection needed. Any current Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari
  will do. It takes a couple of seconds to start; there is a loading bar.


CONTROLS

  WASD .............. move
  Arrow keys ........ aim and fire
  Space ............. fire
  E ................. bomb
  Q ................. use the item you are carrying
  Tab ............... map
  Escape ............ pause

  A gamepad works too: stick or d-pad to move, right trigger to fire, left
  trigger to bomb, A/cross to use.

  All of it is rebindable under Settings -> Controls, from the title screen
  or the pause menu. That screen is also where the volume sliders and the
  accessibility options live.


WHAT YOU ARE PLAYING

  Two chapters of a seven-chapter game. The cellar and the village above it
  are finished and have a real ending; the five floors below them are not
  built yet. A run is roughly fifteen minutes.

  It saves as you go, in the browser's own storage — closing the window and
  opening the file again picks up where you left off.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  Press C at any time. That copies the seed, the character, the items and
  what was happening to your clipboard — paste that into whatever you use to
  tell me about it, and the exact run can be replayed from it.
